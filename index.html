Quick Explain – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Quick Explain is a Chrome extension that shows a short explanation of text you highlight on a web page. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes.

Summary

  • We (the developer) do not collect, store, sell, or share any of your data. The extension has no server of its own, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • When you highlight text, that text is sent to Wikipedia (and to OpenAI or Google Gemini only if you add your own API key for that service) so an explanation can be shown to you.
  • Your settings stay in your own browser.

What is sent, and to whom

Data is sent only when you highlight text on a page (or, if you chose the Alt-key option, highlight text while holding Alt).

DataSent toWhy
The text you highlightedWikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation)To find and show the matching article summary.
The text you highlighted, plus up to about 800 characters of the text around it on the pageOpenAI API and/or Google Gemini API, only if you entered your own API key for that serviceTo generate an explanation. The surrounding text helps pick the right meaning (for example, a movie vs. a book with the same name).

Nothing else from the page is sent: no page address, no browsing history, and no account or personal information. These requests go straight from your browser to those services; the developer never receives them.

Those services handle the data under their own policies: Wikimedia Privacy Policy, OpenAI Privacy Policy (requests are sent with storage turned off), and Gemini API Additional Terms / Google Privacy Policy.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings (answer source, optional OpenAI and Gemini API keys, model names, trigger mode, on/off) are saved with Chrome's local extension storage on your computer. They are not synced or sent to the developer.
  • Recent answers are kept in memory only, so repeated lookups are faster. They are cleared when the browser closes the extension's background process.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties, except to Wikipedia, OpenAI and Google Gemini as described above, solely to provide the extension's feature.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or creditworthiness or lending purposes.
  • We do not load or run code from remote servers.

Your choices

  • Turn the extension off, or switch to the Alt-key trigger, in its settings.
  • Remove your API keys at any time, and only Wikipedia is used.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored settings.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
